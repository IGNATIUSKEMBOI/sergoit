Our Curriculum

Competency Based Curriculum (CBC)

Sergoit Primary School is fully compliant with Kenya's CBC framework, designed to develop learners' competencies — values, skills, and knowledge — needed to flourish in the 21st century.

Our teachers are trained and certified in CBC delivery, with ongoing professional development to ensure every classroom experience is engaging, practical, and learner-centred.

Enroll Your Child
🌱

Pre-Primary Level (PP1 – PP2)

Play-based learning that builds motor skills, language, numeracy, and social development. Ages 4–5.

📘

Lower Primary (Grade 1 – 3)

Literacy, numeracy, environmental activities, creative arts, and movement. Ages 6–8.

🔭

Upper Primary (Grade 4 – 6)

Deeper subject exploration: Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, Science & Technology, Social Studies, Creative Arts. Ages 9–11.

🎓

Junior School (Grade 7 – 9)

Pre-secondary level with specialised subject areas, project work, and KCPE preparation for transition to senior secondary. Ages 12–14.
