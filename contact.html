Admissions Process

Joining Sergoit Primary School is simple. Here's what you need to know.

1

Contact the School

Call or email us to inquire about available spaces for the current or upcoming term.

2

Visit the School

Schedule a visit to tour the facilities, meet the teachers, and understand our approach to education.

3

Submit Documents

Provide birth certificate, immunisation card, previous school leaving certificate (if applicable), and 2 passport photos.

4

Pay Admission Fee

Settle the registration and term fees at our accounts office. M-Pesa Paybill available.

5

Welcome to Sergoit!

Your child receives their uniform list, timetable, and is officially part of the Sergoit family.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What curriculum does Sergoit Primary follow?

We follow the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) as prescribed by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) and the Ministry of Education.

When does each term begin and end?

Kenya's school calendar runs three terms. Dates are set by the Ministry of Education. Please contact us or check the school notice board for exact dates each year.

Does the school offer boarding facilities?

Currently Sergoit Primary operates as a day school. We are exploring options for a boarding section. Contact us for the latest updates.

How can I pay school fees?

Fees can be paid at the accounts office by cash or cheque, or via M-Pesa Paybill. Please contact the school for the current Paybill number.

Are there bursaries or fee assistance programmes?

Yes. We partner with the county government and local NGOs to support deserving students. Speak to the headteacher for more information.

How do I join the school's WhatsApp parent group?

Parents of enrolled students are automatically added to the class WhatsApp groups upon registration. Ask your child's class teacher for the link.
