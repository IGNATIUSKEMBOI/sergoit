Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Core Values

🎯

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, inclusive, and holistic education that empowers every child with knowledge, skills, and values to become a responsible and productive citizen of Kenya and the world.

🌟

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in primary education — producing confident, creative, and compassionate leaders who will shape the future of Kenya and Africa.

🤝

Integrity — honest and principled
Service — to God and community

Our Journey

25 Years of Excellence

From humble beginnings to a thriving school community.

1999

Foundation

Sergoit Primary School was established to serve the growing community of Sergoit in Eldoret, beginning with just 4 classrooms and 3 teachers.

2005

First Major Expansion

Construction of additional classrooms and a staffroom, and the school's first students sat the KCPE examination with remarkable results.

2010

Music & Sports Excellence

The school choir won the regional music festival for the first time, launching a tradition of cultural and sporting achievement.

2015

Computer Lab Established

Donation of a computer lab by community partners and the County Government brought digital literacy to Sergoit learners.

2020

CBC Transition

The school successfully transitioned to the Competency Based Curriculum, training all teachers and revamping the learning environment.

2024

New Classroom Block Commissioned

A state-of-the-art two-storey classroom block with modern facilities was officially opened, accommodating over 400 additional students.
